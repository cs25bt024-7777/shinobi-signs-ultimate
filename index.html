LEARN THE HAND SIGNS

          
SHINOBI SIGN GUIDE

          
Face your palm toward the camera. Hold each shape steady until the game recognizes it.

          

            

              
🐍SNAKE

              
              
Hold four fingers straight and close together. Fold your thumb across your palm; do not stick it out.

              FOUR FINGERS UP
            

            

              
🐯TIGER

              
              
Raise your index and middle fingers together. Curl ring and pinky. Fold your thumb against your palm.

              TWO FINGERS UP
            

            

              
🐴HORSE

              
              
Raise only your index finger. Curl your middle, ring and pinky. Keep your thumb folded in.

              INDEX FINGER ONLY
            

            

              
🐏RAM

              
              
Raise your index and pinky apart. Curl your middle and ring fingers. Keep your thumb folded in.

              INDEX + PINKY UP
            

          

          
TIP: Keep your hand fully inside the webcam frame, palm facing the camera, with good lighting.
